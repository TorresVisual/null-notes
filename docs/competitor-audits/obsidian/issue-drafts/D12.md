# Outgoing-links panel: resolved, unresolved and potential links

## Summary
A panel for the active note listing its links, its unresolved links (with create-note action) and plain-text mentions of other notes' titles that could be linked.

## Problem
Backlinks show who links here, but a student cannot see what the note links to, which links point nowhere, or which existing notes they mention without linking.

## Competitor reference
[Outgoing links](https://obsidian.md/help/plugins/outgoing-links) lists links in the active note and unlinked mentions of other notes' names or aliases.

## Current behavior
`BacklinksPanel.tsx` shows linked and unlinked mentions for the active note; `db` keeps `note_links(source, target_title)`, and `lib/noteLinks.ts` extracts links. There is no outgoing or unresolved-link view.

## Expected behavior
A panel (toggle next to Outline) with three sections: Links, Unresolved, and Possible links with a one-click link action.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Links lists each resolved target once with count; click opens it
- [ ] Unresolved lists link targets with no note; click creates the note (as `[[` click does)
- [ ] Possible links finds text matching other notes' titles (case-insensitive) and converts it to a wiki-link on click
- [ ] Updates as the note changes without blocking typing (reindex path, not keystroke path)
- [ ] Same-title collisions (#122) are shown, not hidden

## Scope
**In:** Per-note outgoing/unresolved/possible links.

**Out (track separately):** Workspace-wide orphan/dead-end reports (could follow), graph filters.

## Implementation considerations
Reuse `BacklinksPanel.tsx` structure and the `note_links` table; the unlinked-mention scan runs on the reindex debounce, not on save.

## Priority
P3. Helps keep a growing workspace connected and finds broken links; the data already exists for backlinks.

Core Editing (linking)
